## Questions Sent

**Q1 (Consistency & earned trust):**
> "Think about the last time you made a commitment to someone on your team — a deadline, a follow-up, a specific piece of support — and then didn't deliver exactly as promised. Not a big betrayal, just a small gap between word and action. What happened? Did the other person ever explicitly call it out, or did they just... absorb it? And here's the harder part: when you think about how that person now shows up with you — how much they trust your word, how willing they are to flag problems with you — does what happened leave a trace? Or put another way: when's the last time someone on your team saw you be completely, boringly consistent — where your actions matched your words so reliably that it was almost unremarkable?"

**Q2 (Genuine allies vs. strategic contacts):**
> "Think about someone in your professional life right now who is genuinely, unmistakably for you — not politically for you, not someone who benefits from your status, but someone who actively wants you to succeed and will tell you hard truths because of that. What makes you certain they're for you? Is it something they did, or something they keep doing? Now the inverse: when's the last time you showed up that way for someone else — where you were consistently, reliably in their corner, including when it cost you something? And be honest about the quality of that reciprocity: is there at least one person who would say with confidence that you're genuinely for them, or are your relationships more transactional than that?"
