# Scoring Rubric

Score each role on a 0-100 scale. Use judgment, but keep the weighting stable.

## Weights

- 0-25: Background match with USER.md (leadership track record, platform scale, domain depth)
- 0-20: Role family alignment with PREFERENCES.md (EM/Director track, agentic/conversational AI domain)
- 0-20: Work model and location fit (Bay Area onsite/hybrid is a near hard-filter — see PREFERENCES.md)
- 0-15: Scope and seniority fit (team size, org level, manager-of-managers vs. first-line)
- 0-10: Skill-signal overlap (agentic AI, RAG, LLM, conversational AI, ML platform, MLOps)
- 0-10: Domain or strategic bonus (AI-native product, platform used at meaningful scale, brand relevance)

## Interpretation

- 85-100: Strong shortlist candidate
- 70-84: Good candidate worth showing
- 55-69: Borderline candidate, include only if the portal is sparse
- Below 55: Exclude unless the user explicitly wants stretch options

## Scoring Guidance

Give high background-match scores when the posting's responsibilities map directly onto Ratul's leadership experience: agentic AI platforms, ranking/retrieval systems at scale, applied ML, and conversational AI.

Give high role-family scores when the title and responsibilities align with the EM/Director role families prioritized in PREFERENCES.md.

Give high work-model and location scores only when a Bay Area onsite or hybrid option is explicitly stated. Remote-only postings should score low here regardless of other fit, per PREFERENCES.md.

Give high scope/seniority scores when the posting confirms real people-management responsibility (not just an IC "Staff/Principal" title) at a scope comparable to or above current role. Reduce this score sharply, or exclude, when management scope cannot be confirmed from the posting.

Use the skill-signal score for explicit mentions of agentic AI, LLM orchestration, RAG, retrieval/ranking, conversational AI, or ML platform/MLOps work.

Use the domain/strategic bonus only when the company's core product or platform is AI-native or operates at meaningful scale, and this is corroborated by the posting.

## Tie-Breakers

Break ties in this order:

1. Confirmed Bay Area onsite/hybrid vs. ambiguous work model
2. Confirmed people-management scope vs. ambiguous scope
3. Stronger domain overlap (agentic AI / conversational AI / RAG) with USER.md
4. Larger or more strategic org scope
